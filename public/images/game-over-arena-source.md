# Game-over artwork

`game-over-arena.webp` is the background for the user's selected Impact game-over concept. All branding, title, live stats, difficulty badge and actions are separate responsive React elements in `GameOverScreen`.

Created with the built-in imagegen tool from the approved mockup on 2026-10-11, then encoded as WebP at quality 88 without resizing. Original size: 1672 × 941. The generated original remains in Codex's generated-images directory.

The left and central 75 percent must be very dark quiet negative space for real HTML typography to be overlaid; leave arena walls and enemy shapes primarily around the outer edges and right side. Absolutely no text, glyphs, labels, lines, buttons, UI panels, logos or watermark. Same art, just erase all interface and lettering. Do not crop or reframe.
